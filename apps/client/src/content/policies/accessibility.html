Our commitment

SoulSeer is committed to providing an accessible website for people with disabilities. We aim to make the information and services at soul-seer.net usable by everyone and to improve accessibility as our website evolves.

Accessibility standard

Our accessibility goal is to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with a range of disabilities. This is an improvement goal, not a claim that every page or feature currently meets all requirements.

Current status and limitations

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We have not established full conformance through a comprehensive accessibility evaluation. Some features, including third-party reading and payment tools, may have limitations.

Feedback and assistance

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, need information in another format, or need assistance using a service, email support@soul-seer.net.

Please include the page address or feature, a description of the difficulty, and your preferred way for us to respond. You may also include your browser or assistive technology if helpful. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or private reading content.

We welcome accessibility feedback and will work with you to identify an accessible way to obtain the information or assistance you need.

Statement updates

We will update this statement as our accessibility work and website change.
